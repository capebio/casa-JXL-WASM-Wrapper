RAW Time-Lapse Studio

Turn a sequence of RAW stills (.ORF, .DNG, .CR2) into a CASAVA (.casv) time-lapse — no ffmpeg / PNG detour. Pick your frames, set the frame rate, and encode. The browser previews the strip (decoded in-page via the WASM RAW pipeline); the full-sequence encode runs the native casv_encode sidecar on the desktop build (each 20 MP frame is too heavy for a whole sequence in the browser heap — see below).
