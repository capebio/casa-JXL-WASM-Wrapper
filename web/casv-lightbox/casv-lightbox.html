CASAVA Video Lightbox

Showcase and inspect CASAVA (.casv) citizen-science video — JPEG XL intra frames plus JOLT REPLACE bbox/tile P-frames. Decode, playback and export run in any browser; native encode and save-to-disk light up in the desktop (Tauri) build. FableBraid (lossless) files show metadata only in the browser — their braided-rANS codec is native-only.
